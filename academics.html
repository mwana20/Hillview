Academics

Strong academic foundations for O-Level and A-Level learners

Academic Overview

Hill View College is committed to providing quality education based on the national curriculum as prescribed by the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB). The school offers both Ordinary Level (UCE) and Advanced Level (UACE) programs, designed to equip learners with knowledge, skills, and values necessary for success in higher education and future careers.

Teaching and learning at Hill View College emphasize understanding, regular assessment, academic discipline, and individual student support. Learners are guided by qualified and experienced teachers who closely monitor progress throughout the academic year.

O-Level Curriculum (UCE)

The O-Level section provides a broad and balanced curriculum aimed at building a strong academic foundation for students. Learners are prepared thoroughly for the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) examinations.

Subjects Offered

  • Mathematics
  • English Language
  • Biology
  • Chemistry
  • Physics
  • Geography
  • History
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Information and Communication Technology (ICT)
  • Agriculture
  • Fine Art
  • Religious Education

Continuous assessment, regular tests, and mock examinations are conducted to evaluate learner progress and improve performance before final examinations.

A-Level Curriculum (UACE)

The A-Level program at Hill View College prepares students for the Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE). The curriculum emphasizes subject specialization, analytical thinking, and academic independence.

Science Combinations

  • Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics (PCM)
  • Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB)
  • Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics (BCM)

Arts Combinations

  • History, Economics, Geography (HEG)
  • Literature, Economics, Geography (LEG)
  • Mathematics, Economics, Geography (MEG)
  • Divinity, Economics, Geography (DEG)

Career guidance and academic counseling are provided to help students choose suitable subject combinations aligned with their future aspirations.

Academic Departments

Academic activities at Hill View College are organized into departments that oversee teaching, assessment, and curriculum implementation.

  • Sciences Department
  • Humanities Department
  • Languages Department
  • ICT Department
  • Creative Arts Department

Academic Calendar

The academic year is divided into three terms. Each term includes classroom instruction, continuous assessment, mid-term tests, and end-of-term examinations.

  • Term I: February – May
  • Term II: June – August
  • Term III: September – December
