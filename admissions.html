Entry Requirements

O-Level Admission

  • Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) results
  • Former school report and recommendation
  • Interview or entrance assessment (where required)

A-Level Admission

  • UCE results with at least two principal passes
  • Combination relevance to intended subjects
  • Interview with the academic committee

Day & Boarding Sections

Hill View College operates both Day and Boarding sections for boys and girls. Boarding students follow a structured routine that includes supervised prep time, meals, and co-curricular activities.

Application Procedure

  1. Pick an application form from the school or website
  2. Attach academic documents and passport photos
  3. Submit to the admissions office
  4. Receive admission confirmation and joining instructions
