Excellence in Academics and Discipline

Hill View College is a mixed day and boarding secondary school offering both O-Level and A-Level education, committed to nurturing disciplined, responsible, and academically excellent learners.

Admissions Contact Us

Welcome to Hill View College

Hill View College is a well-established secondary school providing quality education in a supportive and disciplined environment. The school offers both day and boarding facilities for boys and girls at O-Level and A-Level. We emphasize academic excellence, moral uprightness, leadership, and co-curricular development.

Located in a calm and accessible environment, Hill View College provides learners with modern facilities, qualified teaching staff, and a curriculum that prepares students for national examinations and future careers.

Our Vision

To be a leading secondary school recognized for academic excellence, discipline, and holistic student development at both national and international levels.

Our Mission

To provide quality education through effective teaching, guidance, and mentorship, producing disciplined, skilled, and responsible citizens.

Core Values

  • Discipline
  • Academic Excellence
  • Integrity
  • Respect
  • Teamwork
  • Responsibility

School Leadership

Hill View College is guided by a dedicated and experienced management team committed to academic excellence, discipline, and student welfare.

Director

School Director

The Director provides strategic leadership and ensures that Hill View College maintains high standards of education, discipline, and infrastructure development.

Head Teacher

Head Teacher

The Head Teacher oversees academic programs, staff performance, and student discipline, ensuring that learners are well prepared for both O-Level and A-Level examinations.

Director of Studies

Director of Studies

The Director of Studies coordinates curriculum implementation, examinations, and academic supervision across all classes.

Academic Programs

Hill View College offers a comprehensive curriculum at both O-Level and A-Level, following the national syllabus and emphasizing critical thinking, practical skills, and examination success.

  • O-Level (UCE) curriculum with a wide range of subjects
  • A-Level (UACE) Arts and Science combinations
  • Regular assessments and mock examinations
  • Guidance and career counseling for students
