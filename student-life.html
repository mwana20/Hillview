Overview

Student life at Hill View College is structured to promote academic excellence, discipline, leadership, and personal growth. Both day and boarding students actively participate in school programs.

Boarding Life

Boarding students live in a secure and supportive environment supervised by matrons, wardens, and senior teachers.

  • Well-ventilated dormitories
  • Balanced meals
  • Supervised evening prep
  • Medical and counseling services

Clubs & Societies

  • Science Club
  • Debate & Public Speaking
  • ICT & Robotics Club
  • Music, Dance & Drama
  • Writers & Journalism Club

Sports & Games

Physical education is compulsory for all students. The school participates in inter-school competitions at district and national levels.

  • Football
  • Netball
  • Basketball
  • Volleyball
  • Athletics

Leadership & Discipline

The school operates a strong prefectorial system that trains students in responsibility, leadership, and service.

  • Head Boy & Head Girl
  • Prefects
  • Dormitory Captains
  • Class Monitors

Guidance & Counseling

Hill View College provides guidance and counseling services to support students academically, socially, and emotionally.
