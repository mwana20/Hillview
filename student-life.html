Student Life at Hill View

A vibrant community where academics, sports, arts, and character development come together.

Daily School Routine

Hill View College maintains a structured daily routine that balances academic work, co-curricular activities, and personal development time.

Morning Assembly

7:30 AM - 8:00 AM

Students gather for prayers, announcements, and motivation to start the day.

Academic Sessions

8:00 AM - 3:00 PM

Classes, practical sessions, and educational activities throughout the day.

Sports & Activities

3:30 PM - 5:30 PM

Physical education, club meetings, and co-curricular activities.

Evening Prep

7:00 PM - 9:00 PM

Supervised study time for boarding students to complete assignments.

Sports & Games

Physical fitness and team spirit are essential parts of student life at Hill View College. We offer a variety of sports activities.

  • Football (Boys & Girls)
  • Netball (Girls)
  • Basketball
  • Volleyball
  • Athletics & Track Events
  • Table Tennis

Clubs & Societies

Students can join various clubs and societies to explore their interests and develop new skills.

Debate Club

Develop public speaking, critical thinking, and argumentation skills.

Science Club

Hands-on experiments, projects, and scientific explorations.

Music, Dance & Drama

Express creativity through performing arts and cultural activities.

Religious Fellowship

Spiritual growth and character development through faith-based activities.

Environmental Club

Conservation, gardening, and environmental awareness projects.

ICT Club

Coding, programming, and technology innovation activities.

Boarding Life

Boarding students enjoy a supportive community environment with proper supervision, comfortable accommodation, and balanced meals.

Boarding Facilities Include:

  • Separate dormitories for boys and girls
  • Supervised study areas and prep rooms
  • Spacious dining hall with nutritious meals
  • Common rooms for recreation
  • 24/7 security and medical care
  • Laundry and sanitation facilities

Leadership & Responsibility

Students are encouraged to take on leadership roles through the school prefectorial system and various student committees.

  • Head Prefects (Boy & Girl)
  • Deputy Head Prefects
  • Class Monitors
  • Sports Captains
  • Club Leaders
  • Dormitory Prefects

Student Welfare & Support

Hill View College is committed to the well-being of every student. We provide:

  • Guidance and counseling services
  • Academic mentorship programs
  • Career guidance and university preparation
  • Health and wellness support
  • Discipline and behavior management
  • Parent-teacher communication
